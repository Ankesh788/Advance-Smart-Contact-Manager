What is CommTrace?
CommTrace is a modern Communication Analytics Platform designed to analyse call communication data efficiently. It helps users understand communication patterns, frequently contacted numbers, call duration trends, and behavioral insights through powerful analytics and visual dashboards.
👮
Who Can Use CommTrace?
CommTrace is especially useful for Government agencies, Police departments, Cyber investigation teams, and Data analysts who need quick and accurate insights from large volumes of call data.

⬡ Our Vision
